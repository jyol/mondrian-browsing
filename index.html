Mondrian Browsing

60-second webpage painting

Turn any webpage into a painting

Paint blocks in Mondrian colors in 60 seconds, download your art, and climb the global leaderboard.

Download extension

Install

  1. 1 Unzip mondrian-browsing.zip.
  2. 2 Open chrome://extensions.
  3. 3 Enable Developer mode → Load unpacked → select the folder.
  4. 4 Pin Mondrian Browsing to the toolbar.

How to play

  1. 1 Open any normal webpage.
  2. 2 Click the toolbar icon to start.
  3. 3 Paint as many blocks as you can in 60 seconds.
  4. 4 Enter a nickname for the leaderboard. Download your PNG when done.
